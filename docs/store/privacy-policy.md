# Loungo — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 7 November 2026_

Loungo is a web browser for Android TV. This policy explains what the app does with data.

## Summary
Loungo does not collect, store on any server, sell or share personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics and no advertising SDKs.

## Data on your TV
- **Browsing:** Pages you open are loaded directly from the websites you visit, like in any browser. Those websites have their own privacy policies.
- **Stored only on the TV:** favorites, the last playback position of videos (for "resume"), settings, and a short technical event log (page loads, player errors). This data never leaves the TV unless you choose to share it (see below), and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data. "Clear browsing data" in the phone settings deletes cookies, cache, site data and the event log at any time.
- **Ad blocking:** The app periodically downloads a public filter list. No information about you or your browsing is sent with that request.

## Phone remote
- The phone remote is a web page served by the app on your TV. Your phone connects to the TV directly over your local network.
- Pairing uses a random token stored on the TV and on your phone. No data passes through the internet or any server operated by us.
- The TV announces itself on the local network under a name like `loungo-1234.local` so your phone can find it.
- **Loungo app on an Android phone:** the app is only the TV's remote. It looks for Loungo TVs on your local network and, when you pair, sends your phone's device name to the TV so the TV can ask "Connect this phone?". The name is shown on the TV and not stored or sent anywhere else. The pairing token is kept in the app on your phone.

## "Report a problem"
When you tap "Report a problem" on the phone, the technical event log is shown on your phone and can be shared with your phone's share menu. Nothing is sent automatically; you decide whether and with whom to share it.

## Permissions
- **Internet, network state:** to load web pages and play video.
- **Wi-Fi state, multicast:** to show the pairing address and let your phone find the TV on the local network.
- **Wake lock:** to keep the screen on while a video plays.

## Children
Loungo is a general-purpose browser and is not directed at children under 13.
