## English (default)

**App name / store title** (18): `Loungo: TV Browser` (installed app name: `Loungo`)
Alternative if the title is taken: `Loungo TV Browser` (17)

**Short description** (72):
`Very light, very simple, very fast TV browser. Your phone is the remote.`

**Full description:**

Loungo is a web browser for Android TV. Very light. Very simple. Very fast.

WHY IT IS SO LIGHT
• Closing it really closes it: nothing runs in the background, and it does not start when the TV turns on.
• Your TV's own hardware plays the video: only qualities the TV can decode in hardware are offered, so the processor stays calm.
• One page, no extra load: no tabs by design, and the app rests when the cursor does.
• It lightens itself when the TV struggles: a stuttering page gets its visual effects turned off automatically.

VIDEO, THE WAY IT SHOULD BE
• Loungo finds the video on the page and opens it in a native full-screen player.
• Choose subtitles, audio language and quality from the remote or the phone.
• Subtitles sit in the black bar under wide films, not over the picture.
• Pick up where you left off.

CALM, AD-FREE BROWSING
• Built-in ad and tracker blocking.
• Pop-up and redirect protection.

YOUR PHONE IS THE REMOTE
• Android: install Loungo on your phone too; it finds the TV on the same Wi-Fi. Loungo on the phone is only the remote; the browser is on your TV.
• iPhone: scan the QR code on the TV; the remote opens in your phone's browser.
• Trackpad, keyboard, volume and player controls.
• Fully usable with the TV remote alone.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no analytics, no data collection.
• Your phone talks to your TV directly on your home network.

Requirements: Android TV or Google TV (Android TV 12 or newer). Samsung (Tizen) and LG (webOS) TVs are not supported. Phone remote: the Android app, or any modern phone browser on the same Wi-Fi.
